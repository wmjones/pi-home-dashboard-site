Pi Home Dashboard

A personal, wall-mounted touch screen for one household, running on a Raspberry Pi.

The dashboard shows the household's calendar, the weather, to-do lists and chores, and the music that is currently playing. It runs on a Raspberry Pi on the owner's home network and is used only by the people who live there.

What it connects to

  • Google Calendar (read-only): to show today's and this week's events.
  • Spotify: to show what is playing and offer play, pause and skip.
  • Todoist: to show and check off tasks and chores.
  • Open-Meteo: for the local weather forecast.

This is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
